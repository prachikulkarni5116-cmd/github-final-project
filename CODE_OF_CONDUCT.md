# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and welcoming.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions when an individual represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled confidentially to the extent possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant, depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and other open-source community guidelines.
